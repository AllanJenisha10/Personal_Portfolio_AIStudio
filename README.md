## Overview

This isn't just a portfolio — it's a small case study in how design and engineering intersect. Built entirely with **Google Stitch** and hosted live on **Google AI Studio**, this site was created to tell my story as a B.Tech Information Science and Engineering student at Women's Engineering College, Puducherry (affiliated with Puducherry Technological University) — not through a static resume, but through an experience that reflects how I actually think and build.

## Why This Exists

Most portfolios read like a list — name, degree, skills, projects, done. This one was built to resist that flatness. Every section is meant to feel like a deliberate step in a conversation rather than a bullet point on a page.

The goal was simple: build something that shows *how* I think, not just *what* I've done. A recruiter or collaborator scrolling through shouldn't just see a stack of credentials — they should get a sense of the discipline behind them.

---

## Design Philosophy

The entire visual identity is anchored by one deliberate interaction: the hero photo opens in a **muted duotone filter — navy and bronze** — and shifts to **full color the moment you hover over it**.

It's a small detail, but an intentional one. It mirrors the tagline that drives the whole site:

> Precision first. Color — personality, energy, depth — reveals itself on closer look.

This principle extends beyond the hero section. The layout stays deliberately uncluttered — there's no resume download button, no unnecessary widgets, no decorative noise. Every element earns its place. The site trusts the visitor to explore rather than overwhelming them with everything at once.

## Site Structure

The portfolio is organized into clear, purposeful sections:

1. **Hero / Introduction** — First impression, driven by the duotone-to-color hover interaction and the core tagline
2. **About** — A concise personal and academic introduction
3. **Education** — Full academic history, from school through current B.Tech studies
4. **Academic Performance** — Semester-by-semester SGPA progression
5. **Featured Projects** — Curated selection of technical and design work
6. **Certifications** — Technical and soft-skill credentials earned alongside coursework
7. **Contact** — Direct, frictionless ways to get in touch

## Build Process

The site was designed and assembled in Google Stitch, chosen specifically for its ability to translate design intent directly into a functioning, hosted site without breaking the visual language along the way. Content — bio, education history, project details, certifications — was compiled and structured first, then layered into the Stitch build so that every section had a clear narrative purpose before any visual polish was applied.

---

## A Note on Craft

Every choice on this site — from the tagline to the hover animation to the deliberate absence of clutter — was made to avoid looking templated. It's meant to earn a second look, the same way I try to approach every project: quietly precise, until you look closer.
